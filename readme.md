# Indexanator — Market Snapshots

An almost real time dashboard offering various snapshots of the US stock market: index constituents (S&P 500, NASDAQ-100…) with price, market cap, and daily change, browsable via tabs. Quotes are updated regularly, with a deliberately weird-but-cheap data pipeline.

> **Disclaimer:** Quotes are delayed and informational only. Not for trading purposes. Use at your own risk.
>
> Index names and data are for identification only; "S&P 500" and "NASDAQ-100" are properties of their respective index providers. Data is sourced from public sources and may be inaccurate or delayed.
